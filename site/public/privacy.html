Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What this site collects

  • Design-partner applications: what you enter in the form (name, work email, company, role, company size, what your assistant indexes, your stack and timeline), plus your country as reported by Cloudflare.
  • Abuse prevention: a salted hash of your IP address, used only to limit repeated submissions. We never store the address itself. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person submitted the form; see Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy notice.
  • No analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies of our own.

How it is used

Only to evaluate your application and contact you about the design-partner program. Applications are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and forwarded to the founder through private notification channels. We do not sell or share them.

Retention and deletion

Applications are kept for up to 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them: email hello@ragshield.dev.

The product

The RAG Shield SDK runs in your environment. Your documents are not sent to us.
